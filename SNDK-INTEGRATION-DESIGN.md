## Adapter economics

The standalone source replay matches all 114 saved standard/sensitivity/claim/distribution/terminal cases. Its unfunded operating EV remains explicitly contingent diagnostic evidence. The interactive adapter uses a stricter rule: any opening or annual funding gap withholds all operating/common/per-share values. Unsupported or nonfinite inputs also withhold every value; it never substitutes zero for unknown amounts.

Opening available cash is cash less customer deposits, operating cash, $1,544m tax payables, separate $128m WDC indemnity and selected additional immediate reserves. Optional guarantee calls reduce available cash and equity once. The generic additional reserve enters funding and equity once, never as a duplicate deduction. $590m exhausts default opening surplus; $590.001m produces a gap. Haircut securities are valued separately and never assumed to finance operations. Nonzero debt requires a maturity/interest model and is unsupported.

Annual entered net investment is topped up to own capex + net JV funding − own D&A + positive-growth working capital. This floor is recomputed for every edited annual path and every simulation trial. Top-ups and required investment are shown explicitly. Revenue contraction does not invent a working-capital release. Terminal investment is max(ROIC reinvestment, physical/JV floor + working capital), not their sum. Sustainable terminal FCFF must be positive. Floor ratios are analyst continuity assumptions, not established physical maintenance budgets; lower terminal-floor sensitivities need efficiency justification.

Funded equity is limited-liability floored at zero. Signed economic equity and diagnostic per-share remain separately named fields. The primary per-share fields all use the conditional floored proxy allocation. No securities quote sets a claim value.
