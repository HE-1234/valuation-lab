## Ownership and materiality

- **DUOL:** actual A+B shares exclude treasury shares. Ordinary RSUs are separate from founder awards. Conservatively include all remaining founder shares: 0.72m annual, 0.36m June. Options use reported count, exercise price and remaining life; option volatility/rate are modelling assumptions. No debt.
- **RDDT:** use actual A+B common shares and only unissued RSUs/PRSUs. Restricted shares already issued are not added again (0.090817m annual, 0.064873m June). Reported option intrinsic values remain evidence, but allocation uses DCF common value rather than subtracting a market-price-derived liability. No debt.
- **RKLB:** 45.95125m annual/40.95125m June founder preferred shares have common economic rights. They are common equivalents, not a fixed par-value claim or employee award. Disclosed employee options plus RSUs are conservatively all treated as zero-strike shares: 17.409519m annual and 13.095520m June. The convertible is allocated through a principal-versus-conversion choice, reversing its carrying debt amount first; cash earnout claims 7.634m/13.674m are separate. Capped calls cover 69.26153m shares, strike 5.1255, cap 8.04; maximum intrinsic benefit 201.862729185m. June collared forwards require 7.4512m future shares and $474m–$642m expected April 2028 proceeds, with no cash received at June. The source-backed conservative proxy discounts the minimum proceeds at 4%, assumes April 30 settlement, and deducts the maximum 2% fee: $432.2740257m other assets, accompanied by all future shares. Upper proceeds stress is $585.4850728m. June current finance lease carries the annual $0.317m balance: even a 100% error is below $0.001/share. The standalone case excludes the pending $8bn Iridium transaction. The October 14 filing delayed Neutron's first launch into 2027; the growth and profitability path remain scenarios.
- **CRWD:** both bases use the July 2026 four-for-one split basis, with same-date January comparative shares and awards from the July filing. No EPS weighted-average denominator. Existing RSUs/PSUs and fixed founder holdbacks are included separately. Noncontrolling interest is deducted against consolidated strategic investments. Senior debt is not convertible. Reported loss remains the starting operating income; no blanket SBC addback.
- **PANW:** FY2026 contains only part-year CyberArk and Chronosphere earnings but full acquisition balance sheets. $1,774m convertible debt is already carried at fair value. Include its paired $153m capped-call hedge in other assets; do not add a second fair-value premium or conversion shares. Additional claims total $611m: $206m acquisition earnout plus $405m vested, liability-classified deferred compensation. All old options/warrants were settled; unvested RSU/PSU target count totals 25m. Principal instead of fair-value debt would increase equity by $677m before dilution, disclosed as a material model sensitivity.
- **APP:** recent flows exclude disposed Apps operations; earlier historical years have different business scope. Tripledot equity-method investment plus nonmarketable investments are added as nonoperating assets. June's complete finance-lease liability is not separately reported: annual $122.653m minus first-half $8.528m principal produces $114.125m proxy. A $60.319m stress for unclassified new leases changes value by less than $0.18/share. Annual option and RSU/PSU stocks are carried to June, about 0.76% of actual common shares; this is a disclosed conservative proxy, not a June reported award rollforward.
