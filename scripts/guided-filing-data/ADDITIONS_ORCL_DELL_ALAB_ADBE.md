## Material treatments and remaining limits

### ORCL

Enterprise databases and cloud applications, with a rapidly expanding, capital-intensive AI cloud infrastructure business.

- The default keeps recent AI buildout intensity in the explicit forecast: sales-to-new-capital of 0.22 means about $4.55 of net reinvestment per $1 of incremental revenue. With current debt and other claims, this scenario produces negative modeled common equity, displayed at a zero floor. It is a conservative conditional scenario, not an assertion that Oracle is worthless. Post-buildout capital efficiency, customer funding and terminal assumptions materially change the result; historical book capital is contextual and is not substituted for future investment.
- August 2026 restricted cash of $2,565m is excluded from excess cash. The 141m ATM shares and $19.9bn net proceeds are already in the August balance sheet and are not added again.
- Short-term capex financing has no standalone reported closing balance. A conservative claim proxy assumes zero opening at FY2024 and accumulates net financing of $1,422m + $3,345m = $4,767m for May 2026 and subtracts $830m to reach $3,937m for August. The notes-payable schedule reconciles separately and excludes these cash flows. An omitted $1bn opening balance would reduce value by about $0.33/share; the full proxy is about $1.3–1.6/share.
- The 31.238285m maximum mandatory preferred-conversion shares are an explicit conservative current-value proxy for a future market-price collar; the minimum 24.99063m sensitivity is available. Preferred carrying amount of $4,954m is not also subtracted. Remaining cumulative dividends are discounted separately at a 6.5% analyst rate; nominal payments provide a sensitivity.
- Annual 81m restricted awards and 21m options are carried into August because no complete interim rollforward is disclosed; 2.4m new awards were exceeded by 4m forfeitures/cancellations before vesting or exercises. Carrying annual counts is conservative, not an exact current count.
- Uncommenced data-center leases are $288bn at August and purchase obligations total $34.15bn. They are future operating and investment commitments, not added mechanically to current debt. Growth, margin and reinvestment must fund the capacity; contracted revenue can fail to translate into cash if customers default or construction slips.
- Net pension liability of $256m is included once and carried from May to August. Matched deferred-compensation investments and liabilities are excluded. Nonmarketable investment carrying amounts of $2.3bn/$2.4bn is a valuation proxy; a 50% haircut changes value by roughly $0.4/share.
- FY2017/2018 were retrospectively restated for Topic 606 and pension presentation; FY2016 opening context was not. The transition is excluded from comparable historical growth/capital ranges. History is latest-reported context, not a point-in-time backtest.
- Gross uncertain income-tax exposures and related interest/penalties total $13.2bn at May 2026. They are not all assumed immediately payable or deducted as debt; the timing and ultimate settlement remain a material tax sensitivity beyond the normalized 20% operating tax scenario.
- Customer prepayment funding reserve is 4,592m for May and 15,955m for August, shown separately from debt and restricted cash. Full, half and omitted reserve scenarios expose a material cash-conversion uncertainty. The reserve is a conservative gross cash-flow accumulation, not a separately reported closing balance.

History: 10 displayed annual rows; opening period FY2016.

### DELL

AI servers, storage, networking and personal computers, with an integrated customer-financing business.

- Whole-enterprise FCFF includes DFS financing income in revenue and subtracts all consolidated corporate and DFS debt once. DFS borrowing interest is below EBIT. Financing receivables and customer leased equipment are operating capital, never added again as surplus investments.
- FY2026 book equity is negative after repurchases. Large payables and supplier-finance balances support working capital; use the explicit reinvestment assumption rather than treating a high book sales/capital ratio as free growth. Supplier finance of $1.98bn annual and about $3.1bn July remains trade payables, as filed, without a second debt deduction.
- July carries 19 million annual RSU/PSU units because the interim filing omits a complete award roll-forward; PSUs are at target, with possible 0%-200% vesting. A +/-25% total-award scenario tests roughly 0.7% of allocation shares. Fractional rounding and small deferred units are below about 0.1% of shares.
- The 2.5 million COO options at $141.77 require market-capitalization, adjusted free-cash-flow and service conditions. The model conservatively assumes full vesting and values contractual time remaining at the modeled share price; $132m grant-date fair value is not deducted again.
- The $248m international pension deficit is included once as a separate claim and carried to July; a 50% change moves value by about $0.19 per share. Annual finance leases are disclosed as immaterial; $100m omitted debt would move value about $0.15 per share.
- Strategic investments of $1.73bn annual and $2.679bn July are added once at carrying value; private securities may realize different prices. The $0.6bn July YTD investment gains sit below EBIT and are not projected as operating earnings.

History: 10 displayed annual rows; opening period FY2016.

### ALAB

Connectivity semiconductors that link processors, accelerators and memory inside AI and cloud infrastructure; growth depends on customer platform adoption and concentration.

- The business has four available annual periods. Pre-IPO preferred stock and the 2024 IPO prevent comparable pre/post-IPO capital calibration; the 2024 IPO-triggered stock compensation remains in GAAP operating history.
- Unquantified noncurrent investments are omitted, not zero-filled. Other assets less operating-lease ROU assets provide conservative ceilings of $54.945m at year-end and $32.667m at June, below $0.31 per allocation share. The annual ceiling still includes goodwill and other operating assets; these are ceilings, not investment estimates.
- February 2026 acquisition holdback 5 m is deducted once; issued acquisition shares are already in common stock. Existing customer-warrant revenue reductions and ongoing stock compensation remain in the operating margin.

History: 4 displayed annual rows; opening period unavailable. Only four real annual income-statement periods (FY2022–FY2025) are available in the IPO and annual filings; no earlier annual results are invented.

### ADBE

Creative, document and enterprise marketing software subscriptions, with AI tools expanding workflows while creating new competition and inference costs.

- Semrush acquiredApril 28,2026 for 1.874 bn is already included in the August balance sheet, current awards and results from closing; no second purchase-price deduction. Topaz Labs 340 m was agreed but unclosed atAugust 28 and is excluded from the dated base; its gross cash commitment is about$0.84 per allocation share before acquired value.
- Private investment carrying values are not separately quantified and receive no extra asset value. The entire other-assets balance less matched plan assets is only 1.268 bn annual or 1.355 bn interim, a conservative ceiling below$3.4 per allocation share that also contains non-investment operating assets.
- Unvested awards use full RSU vesting plus target performance shares:8.5 m annual and 13.4 m interim. Maximum 200% performance payout would add only 0.6 m shares (about 0.15% of the interim denominator). The annual 12 m net deferred-compensation funding gap is carried as a small interim proxy (~$0.03/share).
- The FY 2024 Figma termination fee 1 bn remains in reported historical operating income; it is not projected as recurring. FY 2026 goodwill impairment 70 m and acquisition costs remain in current GAAP earnings.
