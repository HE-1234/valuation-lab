Cutoff: 2026-10-21. This review implements the user's permission to use visible proxies for small uncertainties. It does not turn an undisclosed amount into a reported fact.

June other claims include the Avride SAFE 178.1, Tavily holdback/earnout 19.0, **Eigen deferred purchase consideration 24.3**, and NCI 1.0. The Eigen purchase obligation was identified in Notes 3 and 4 during this review; it is distinct from employment-conditioned future compensation.

Use the disclosed convertible fair value by replacing its carrying value once. Do not also add conversion shares. This is a dated fair-value proxy, not a re-estimation of conversion economics for every operating scenario. The June premium alone is about $41.20 per pre-option modeled share and is consequential.

Parent options can use a Black–Scholes sensitivity model. Annual counts, exercise prices and remaining contractual lives are reported; annual grant assumptions support 60% volatility and a 4.2% midpoint risk-free rate. June count and weighted strike are reported, while annual volatility/rate and a rolled-forward 7.8-year contractual life are transparent proxies. The free-share alternative bounds the total option dilution at approximately 2.75% annually and 2.20% for June before other interactions.

## Avride proxy and limits

The annual filing discloses a 7.926674m-share authorized Avride pool representing 20% of fully diluted subsidiary capital. It still does not provide a current economic value of outstanding awards. The October SAFE converts at an 80% discount factor to a future financing price; the $375m announced investment/commitment package is **not** an equity valuation. Unamortized compensation also is not current award value. The old $14.50 RSU grant price should not be carried across the 2025 subsidiary reorganization as if comparability were proved.

A $100m reserve is a chosen analyst allowance. Each $100m moves value by at most $0.3865 per annual-base share or $0.3343 per June-base share before parent-option response. The JSON includes $0m, $500m and $1,000m reserve scenarios. These are **not a proven upper bound**. At $10bn positive equity, a $1bn claim would be 10%; at $60bn it would be 1.67%. Do not conclude that the claim is immaterial because the current default hits zero.

## Operating issue remains consequential

The continuing-operation perimeter is identifiable: Nebius AI cloud, Avride and TripleTen. Disposed Yandex operations are excluded, and Toloka operating results are discontinued with the retained stake handled as an investment. This supports removing a blanket unknown-perimeter gate.

It does not validate the operating forecast. A slow ten-year fade from the unusually negative historical margin generates enormous funding needs. The current defaults should be labelled a **slow-transition funding stress**, not a base fair-value estimate.

The staged illustration uses EBIT margins -15%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 22%, then 25%, retaining the original growth, reinvestment and discount assumptions. The comparison isolates the effect of profitability timing; it is not management guidance. Both cases still leave common equity at the limited-liability zero floor after the ownership bridge.

Primary guidance supports FY2026 revenue of $3.0–3.4bn, approximately 40% adjusted EBITDA margin, and $7–9bn exit ARR. Adjusted EBITDA excludes depreciation and share compensation; ARR is a last-month annualization. The $3.2bn recognized-revenue midpoint implies approximately 504% growth from the annual 2025 base, exceeding the existing engine's 200% yearly limit. Do not silently cap it or change the historical denominator. A defensible calendar ramp needs explicit capex, depreciation, customer-advance movements and operating assumptions, beyond simply clearing the ownership gate.

Negative FCFF already charges modeled reinvestment. Do not deduct the same financing need a second time. The dated valuation assumes external financing can be raised on fair terms; financing fees, distress and below-fair-value equity issuance can hurt existing owners further. Later financing is excluded from the dated cash and share bridge. Gross customer prepayments cannot be treated as free revenue without accounting for future service delivery and revenue recognition.
